### Documents

- The texts Exegete gives the assistant were read in full, as the
  documents were in 0.15.2: the descriptions of the tools this release
  does not otherwise change, the brief, the prompts, the resources, and
  the notes and refusals in the answers. The rest follow when those
  tools' changes are in.
- They no longer take every document for an interview. The rules on
  evidence speak of the same person or document; the rules for
  interviews apply to interviews. A coded passage can be "a whole
  paragraph or answer" long, and the examples vary.
- They no longer take it that you use QualCoder. A copy or a backup
  taken while another program is writing to the project holds what was
  last saved. Putting an existing code under another code is not in
  Exegete yet, and is done in QualCoder, if you use it.
- Three untrue sentences were corrected. The export of coded segments
  said it follows a "File > Reports" menu, which QualCoder does not
  have: it follows the coding report, Code retrieval in QualCoder 4.0's
  Analysis menu. The tool that sets the AI coder name said Exegete
  compares code names as QualCoder 4.0 does, where QualCoder's own
  dialogs compare them letter for letter. A merge of categories credited
  a note in the target's memo to QualCoder's development branch, though
  QualCoder 4.0 writes it.
- Capitals for emphasis, emoji and phrases such as "Perfect for" are
  gone, and the texts call the person whose study it is "the
  researcher" throughout.
- Sentences that packed several facts together were split, one idea
  at a time, and words from Exegete's own code, such as "base tables",
  "echo" and "reparent", gave way to plain ones. The rules the texts
  state are the same.
