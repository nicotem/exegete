# Earlier documents

Early plans, analyses and research notes, and the early security review
with the fixes that followed it, kept for the history of Exegete's
design. Each is marked historical at its top, and none describes Exegete
as it is now: all were written in October 2025, when it was called
qualcoder-mcp, and much of what they plan or describe has since changed
or been removed. The living documents are at the top of the repository:
the [README](../../README.md), [TOOLS.md](../../TOOLS.md) and the
[CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md) say what Exegete does today.

- Plans: AI_CODING_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md, PHASE_2_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md and FEATURE_ANALYSIS.md.
- Analyses and research notes: IMPORT_EXPORT_ANALYSIS.md, QUALCODER_IMPORT_CAPABILITIES.md and RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md.
- Instructions for the export and import workflow of v0.3.0, since removed: IMPORT_INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- The security review and the fixes that followed it: SECURITY_REVIEW.md and SECURITY_FIXES.md.
